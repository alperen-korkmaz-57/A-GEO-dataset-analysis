Tidying the data.

The GEO dataset contains various types of information; we have specific functions to extract each of them individually: exprs() .Retrieves the expression dataset from the eSet object. pData() .Retrieves clinical/metadata regarding the samples (phenotype data); information on experimental phenotypes recorded in eSet and ExpressionSet-derived classes. fData() .Retrieves information on features recorded in eSet-derived classes (feature data); extracts gene and annotation information related to the probes. experimentData() and annotation() .Contain general information—such as who conducted the experiment, the publication date, the PubMed ID, and the laboratory protocols used. These are important for reporting purposes rather than for data analysis.
